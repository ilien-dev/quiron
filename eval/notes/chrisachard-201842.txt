- #1 question author gets about consulting: finding clients; felt it himself starting out
- most contracts come via people you know -> meet people in your field (not "networking")
- local language meetups: attend, lightning talk after a few, full talk months later
- need public proof of expertise: portfolio/blog/ebook (ebooks work great), YouTube, podcast
- tell contacts you're looking; ask who they know, not whether their company needs help
- most of author's past clients = friends-of-friends
- hiring posts in your network = chance to pitch contracting; may pay off months/years later
- subcontracting for consultancies: less pay, fewer hassles; target 5–10 person shops; many have overflow work
- need work fast: avoid big freelance marketplaces; use tech job boards, email hiring manager (not HR), offer contract instead of full-time
- smaller companies = less red tape; many ignore/decline but author surprised how many say yes
- on "no": ask for referrals; tech world is small
- a few clients = good year; not volume sales
