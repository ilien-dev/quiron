Stories like this remind us why we build what we build, and we would love to feature yours next. Just reply to this email to get in touch.

**Tips and Tricks**

Did you know you can customize your notification settings by project instead of managing them globally? Open Settings, choose Notifications, and select "Per Project" to quiet the noise on low-priority work while staying alert on urgent tasks. Another favorite among our power users is keyboard shortcuts. Press the question mark key anywhere in the app to see the full list and speed up your daily routine.
