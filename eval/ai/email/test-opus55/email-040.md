Dear Committee Members,

Thank you for your continued dedication to bringing the 2012 Summer Olympic Games to Houston. As we move into the next phase of our bid, I want to share a brief update and outline our priorities for the coming months.

First, our venue planning team has completed its preliminary review of proposed sites. The Reliant Park complex remains central to our plan, and we are finalizing transportation studies connecting downtown, the Medical Center, and the proposed Olympic Village. These studies will be essential when the evaluation commission visits.

Second, community support continues to grow. Local businesses, universities, and civic organizations have pledged their backing, and our volunteer registry has passed several thousand names. I encourage each of you to keep promoting the bid within your networks, as public enthusiasm will weigh heavily in the final decision.

Third, we must strengthen our financial guarantees. The finance subcommittee will meet next Thursday to review sponsorship commitments and the draft operating budget. Please send any outstanding documents to the bid office before then.

Finally, I would like to schedule a full committee meeting later this month to review our presentation materials. A calendar invitation will follow shortly.

Houston has the spirit, diversity, and infrastructure to host a world-class Games. With your continued hard work, I am confident we can show the world what our city has to offer.
