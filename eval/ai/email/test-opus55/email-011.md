Dear Members,

The Business Issues Committee is now accepting nominations for the position of Vice-Chairperson for the upcoming term. This is an exciting opportunity for an engaged and forward-thinking member to help shape the direction of our work and to strengthen the voice of the business community we serve.

Over the past year, the Committee has made meaningful progress on several fronts. We have hosted policy roundtables with local officials, published recommendations on regulatory streamlining, and launched a mentorship initiative connecting established business owners with emerging entrepreneurs. As we look ahead, we want to build on that momentum, and the Vice-Chairperson will play a central role in doing so.

**Role and Responsibilities**

The Vice-Chairperson works closely with the Chairperson to guide the Committee's agenda and activities. Key responsibilities include:

- Presiding over meetings in the Chairperson's absence
- Helping set priorities and coordinate subcommittee efforts
- Representing the Committee at community events and stakeholder meetings
- Supporting member recruitment and engagement
- Assisting in the preparation of reports, position statements, and annual reviews

The position requires a commitment of approximately five to eight hours per month, including attendance at monthly Committee meetings and occasional special sessions.

**Who Should Apply**

We welcome candidates from all industries and business sizes. Ideal candidates will:

- Have been active members of the Committee for at least one year
- Demonstrate strong communication and collaboration skills
- Bring a genuine interest in economic development and policy issues
- Be willing to listen to diverse perspectives and help build consensus

Prior leadership experience is helpful but not required. What matters most is enthusiasm, reliability, and a commitment to our shared goals.

**How to Apply**

To be considered, please submit a brief statement of interest (no more than one page) describing your background, your reasons for seeking the role, and the priorities you would bring to the Committee. Self-nominations are encouraged, and members may also nominate a colleague with that person's consent.
