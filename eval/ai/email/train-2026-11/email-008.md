Hi everyone,

We’re delighted to welcome our newest colleagues to the team. Starting a new role is an exciting milestone, and we want every new starter to feel supported, informed, and included from their very first day.

Over the coming weeks, our new team members will be getting to know the business, meeting colleagues across departments, and learning about the projects and priorities that shape our work. Please take a moment to introduce yourself, explain what your team does, and help them find their feet.

For our new starters, your induction will include introductions to key contacts, guidance on our systems and processes, and time with your manager to discuss your responsibilities and initial goals. You’ll also receive information about any required training and the resources available to support you.

Please ask questions whenever you need to. There is plenty to take in during the first few weeks, and nobody expects you to know everything immediately. Whether you need help accessing a system, understanding a process, or finding the right person to speak to, your manager and colleagues are here to help.

We encourage existing team members to include our new colleagues in relevant meetings, informal conversations, and team activities. Small gestures, such as inviting someone for a coffee or checking in after their first week, can make a real difference.

Managers, please ensure that induction arrangements are in place and that regular check-ins are scheduled. It’s important that each new starter understands what success looks like and has the opportunity to share feedback about their experience.

To our newest colleagues: we’re pleased you’re here and look forward to getting to know you. Your experience, ideas, and perspectives will be a valuable addition to the team.

Thank you to everyone helping make this a warm and welcoming start. We’re looking forward to working together and celebrating your contributions as you settle into your roles.
