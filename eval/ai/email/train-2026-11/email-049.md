Effective Monday, November 17, all direct trades must be verbally confirmed with the counterparty before they are booked. This applies to every trade arranged outside our electronic platforms, including phone, chat, and email negotiations.

Over the past quarter, we've had several breaks caused by mismatched details on direct trades, mostly incorrect quantities, settlement dates, and price conventions. Each one was resolved, but they cost time, created avoidable exposure, and in two cases required corrections after settlement. A short verbal check at the point of execution would have caught all of them.

**What's required:**

1. **Confirm the key terms aloud.** Before booking, read back the instrument, direction, quantity, price, trade date, and settlement date to the counterparty and get explicit agreement.
2. **Use a recorded line.** Confirmations must take place on a recorded phone line so we have an auditable record. Mobile phones and unrecorded lines do not qualify.
3. **Log the confirmation.** Note the time of the call and the name of the person you spoke with in the trade ticket's confirmation field.
4. **Escalate discrepancies immediately.** If any term doesn't match, do not book the trade. Resolve it with the counterparty, and flag it to your desk head if it can't be settled on the call.

Written confirmations will still be issued as usual. The verbal step is an additional control, not a replacement.

Operations will begin spot-checking tickets for the confirmation log from the start of next week. If you have questions about how this applies to your desk, or foresee any practical issues, please raise them with me before Friday.
