**Something to try this month**

Choose one task, habit, or project you’ve been meaning to move forward. Instead of setting a large goal, identify one action you can complete in fifteen minutes. You might organize your notes, send an introductory message, outline a plan, or ask someone for advice.

Once you’ve finished, decide on the next small step. Progress often feels more manageable when you give yourself a clear starting point and permission to build gradually. Consider inviting a friend or colleague to try the same exercise and compare what you learn.
