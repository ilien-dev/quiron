Please find below the summary of environmental activities for the Roswell area for the week ending November 12, 2026.

**1. Air Quality Monitoring**

Air quality remained within acceptable limits across all monitoring stations this week. Particulate matter (PM2.5) averaged 9 µg/m³, well below the regulatory threshold. Ozone levels peaked briefly on Tuesday afternoon but stayed under the eight-hour standard. No exceedances were recorded, and all instruments passed their routine calibration checks.

**2. Water Resources and Quality**

Field staff collected samples from six surface water and two groundwater sites. Preliminary results show pH, turbidity, and dissolved oxygen within normal seasonal ranges. Laboratory analysis for nitrates and trace metals is pending and expected by early next week. Groundwater levels in the monitoring wells along the Pecos River corridor were stable, with only minor seasonal decline.

**3. Waste Management and Site Inspections**

Inspectors completed four scheduled site visits, covering two construction sites, one light industrial facility, and the municipal transfer station. Minor housekeeping issues were noted at one construction site, including uncovered stockpiles and incomplete sediment barriers. The contractor was notified and has committed to corrective action by Friday. Hazardous waste manifests were reviewed and found to be complete and accurate.

**4. Wildlife and Habitat**

Biologists conducted routine habitat surveys along the river corridor. Migratory bird activity increased, with sandhill cranes observed in larger numbers near the wildlife refuge. No injured or stranded wildlife was reported. Invasive plant removal continued along the riverbank, with approximately half an acre cleared this week.

**5. Community Outreach**

The team hosted a recycling awareness session at the community center, attended by roughly 40 residents. Questions focused on household hazardous waste disposal and drought-tolerant landscaping. Feedback was positive, and additional materials will be distributed at the public library next week.

**6. Compliance and Permitting**

Two stormwater permit renewals were reviewed and forwarded for approval. One notice of violation remains open pending the contractor's corrective action report. No new enforcement actions were initiated.
