I pray for your strength. There are moments when we feel tired in ways that sleep cannot fix, when the road seems longer than we expected. In those moments, I hope you find renewed energy, whether it comes from a kind word, a moment of stillness, or the support of people who love you. May you be reminded that you are more resilient than you realize.

Take care of yourself, and be gentle with your heart. You are valued more than you know.
