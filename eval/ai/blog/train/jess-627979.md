# SheCoded 2021 — Stories From Women Building Software and the Allies Supporting Them

Every year, it becomes increasingly clear that the tech industry needs more diverse voices and perspectives. The SheCoded 2021 event brought together women in software development and the allies who support them to share stories, experiences, and insights. This gathering celebrates the achievements of women in tech while also acknowledging the challenges that remain and the importance of community support.

## What is SheCoded?

SheCoded is an annual event that provides a platform for women in technology to share their experiences, learn from one another, and connect with allies in the industry. The event features talks, panels, workshops, and networking opportunities that focus on the unique experiences and perspectives of women in software development.

The 2021 iteration was particularly special, bringing together a diverse group of speakers from different backgrounds, experience levels, and specializations. Some were senior engineers with decades of experience, while others were just beginning their careers in tech.

## Stories of Inspiration

One of the most powerful aspects of SheCoded is the personal stories shared by attendees. Women spoke about their paths into tech—many describing non-traditional routes into the industry. Some came from liberal arts backgrounds, others transitioned from completely different careers, and many described the support systems that enabled them to break into an industry that can sometimes feel unwelcoming.

A recurring theme throughout the event was the importance of mentorship and community. Women described how finding other women in tech—whether in their organizations, through conferences, or online—made a tremendous difference in their careers. These connections provided both practical guidance and emotional support in navigating an industry where women remain underrepresented, particularly in senior positions and technical roles.

## Overcoming Challenges

The event didn't shy away from discussing real challenges. Speakers openly discussed impostor syndrome, workplace bias, work-life balance, and the unique pressures women face in tech. Notably, these weren't framed as personal failings but as systemic issues that the industry needs to address.

One particularly moving panel discussed the experience of being one of the few women (or the only woman) in the room. Panelists shared strategies for speaking up, advocating for themselves, and supporting other women in similar situations. The conversation acknowledged that while individual resilience matters, systemic change is necessary.

## The Role of Allies

A significant portion of SheCoded 2021 was dedicated to discussing how allies can support women in tech. Male engineers, managers, and executives shared their perspectives on creating inclusive teams, actively listening to concerns about bias and harassment, and using their privilege to amplify women's voices.

Allies described practical steps they take: ensuring diverse hiring panels, providing mentorship to junior women engineers, calling out inappropriate behavior, advocating for equal pay and advancement opportunities, and creating psychological safety within their teams.

The message was clear: supporting women in tech isn't just the responsibility of women themselves. It requires commitment from everyone in the industry.

## Career Paths and Growth

Beyond challenges, the event celebrated the diverse career paths women in tech have taken. Some spoke about staying in individual contributor roles because they loved coding and problem-solving. Others described their journeys into management and leadership. Still others discussed their transitions into specialized fields like security, DevOps, or machine learning.

A common thread was the importance of choice and autonomy. Rather than prescribing a "correct" career path, SheCoded emphasized that women should have the freedom to define success on their own terms, whether that's technical depth, leadership, entrepreneurship, or any combination thereof.

## Building Community

SheCoded emphasized that community is crucial. Whether through local meetups, online groups, conferences, or workplace initiatives, connecting with other women in tech provides both practical and emotional support. Women talked about forming study groups, starting their own companies, mentoring younger women, and creating safe spaces to discuss challenges and celebrate wins.

## Moving Forward

The 2021 event concluded with a call to action. For women in tech, this meant encouraging continued visibility, support for one another, and advocacy for themselves and their peers. For allies, it meant redoubling efforts to create inclusive environments and actively work toward systemic change.

## Conclusion

SheCoded 2021 was a celebration of women in software development and an acknowledgment that while progress has been made, more work remains. The event showcased the talent, diversity, and resilience of women in tech while also making it clear that creating a truly inclusive industry requires commitment from everyone. The stories shared—of achievement, perseverance, community, and support—serve as inspiration for anyone working toward a more diverse and welcoming tech industry. Whether you attended the event or are learning about it for the first time, the message is powerful: women belong in tech, and everyone benefits when women are valued, supported, and included.
