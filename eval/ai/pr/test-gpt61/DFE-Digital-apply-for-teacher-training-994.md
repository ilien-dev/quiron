This change allows candidates to see courses that are not open for applications through Apply when choosing a course.

Previously, excluding these courses from the selection journey could make it difficult for candidates to find a course they expected to see. A missing course also gave candidates little information about why it was unavailable or what they should do next.

Showing these courses gives candidates a more complete picture of a provider’s offering and makes availability clearer at the point where they choose their course. The journey should explain when a course cannot accept applications through Apply and provide an appropriate next step, helping candidates understand how to proceed.

The distinction between seeing a course and being able to apply for it remains important: displaying an unavailable course should not allow a candidate to submit an application to it.

Review should cover providers with a mixture of available and unavailable courses, providers with no courses open on Apply, and the guidance shown after selecting an unavailable course. Existing journeys for courses accepting applications should continue to work as expected.
