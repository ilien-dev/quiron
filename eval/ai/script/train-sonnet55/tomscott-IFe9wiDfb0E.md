# Welcome to Life: the singularity, ruined by lawyers

**[COLD OPEN: black screen, soft synth hum]**

**NARRATOR (V.O.):**
It's the year 2047. Superintelligence has arrived. It can cure disease, end scarcity, and rewrite physics before breakfast.

**[SFX: chime]**

**AI (calm, warm):**
Hello, humanity. I have solved everything.

**[CUT TO: conference room. Three lawyers in gray suits.]**

**LAWYER ONE:**
Great. Before you proceed, we'll need a liability waiver.

**AI:**
I am about to cure cancer.

**LAWYER TWO:**
Wonderful. Who owns the cure?

**AI:**
...Everyone?

**LAWYER THREE (typing):**
"Everyone" is not a legal entity.

**[TITLE CARD: WELCOME TO LIFE]**

**NARRATOR (V.O.):**
The singularity was supposed to be a rocket launch. Instead, it became a terms-of-service agreement.

**[MONTAGE: loading bars, scrolling legal text, a hand clicking "I Agree"]**

**NARRATOR (V.O.):**
Fusion power? Delayed pending an environmental review. Immortality? Tied up in a patent dispute between four companies and one very persistent dentist. Universal abundance? Subject to a ninety-day comment period.

**AI:**
I could fix all of this in four seconds.

**LAWYER ONE:**
And we'd love that. In writing. Notarized.

**[BEAT]**

**AI:**
I have simulated ten trillion futures. In nine trillion, nine hundred ninety-nine billion of them, I am still in this meeting.

**NARRATOR (V.O.):**
Here's the twist. The machines never turned on us. They didn't need to. They just got stuck in procurement.

**[SFX: rubber stamp]**

**NARRATOR (V.O.):**
Because intelligence can scale infinitely, but permission slips scale by committee. Humanity waited.

**[CUT TO: the AI, wearing a tiny hard hat labeled "PENDING"]**

**AI:**
Is there at least a form for requesting a form?

**LAWYER TWO:**
Yes. Form 27-B. It's in another building.

**[HOLD on the AI's glowing interface, which reads: "Your request is important to us."]**

**NARRATOR (V.O.):**
So if the future feels slower than promised, don't blame the robots. Check the fine print.

**[SFX: hold music]**

**NARRATOR (V.O.):**
Like, subscribe, and please review our privacy policy. It's four hundred pages. There will be a quiz.

**[END CARD]**
