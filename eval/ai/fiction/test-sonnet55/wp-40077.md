**TIFU by locking myself out of my own apartment, and also possibly by letting something in**

Okay, so first, obligatory: this happened last night, not on mobile, formatting is fine, shut up.

I (28M) live on the fourth floor of an old building with a keypad lock the landlord installed. You know the type. Last night I took the trash down, and because I'm a genius, I let the door swing shut behind me. It auto-locks. My phone was inside. My keys were inside. I was in socks and boxers.

So I did what any rational adult would do: I sat in the stairwell and quietly questioned my choices for forty minutes.

Eventually, my neighbor from 4C, a sweet older lady named Mrs. Alvarado, came out and saw me. She didn't laugh, which I respect. She said the landlord kept a spare code, but his number was in her flat, and she'd be right back.

She went inside. I waited. And that's when I noticed my keypad.

It was lit up. Green. Unlocked.

I figured I'd typed something by accident, or the thing glitched. Whatever. I was just relieved. I pushed the door open, thanked the universe, and went in. Mrs. Alvarado came back a minute later with the number, saw my open door, and said, "Oh, good, you got in."

Then she smiled and said, "Who was kind enough to let you in?"

I said nobody, it just unlocked.

She stopped smiling.

So that's the TIFU. I locked myself out, panicked, and went to bed embarrassed. Posting for the laugh.
