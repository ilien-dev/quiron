# TIFU by answering the door at 3am

TIFU by answering my apartment door at 3am for a stranger who is now, as far as I can tell, living in my walls.

So context: I live alone, fourth floor walkup, and I've got a bad habit of not checking the peephole because my building has genuinely never had anything worse than a drunk neighbor knock on the wrong door. Last night there's knocking, soft, almost polite, and half-asleep me just opens it like an idiot.

There's a woman standing there. Maybe sixty, maybe eighty, hard to tell, wearing a cardigan that's buttoned wrong, and she says, very calmly, "I think I left the stove on," and then just... stands there. Not my stove. Not my problem, technically, except she won't leave, and she's not agitated, just patient, like she's waiting for me to solve something.

I ask if she needs me to call someone. She says "he doesn't answer anymore" and smiles in a way that makes my arms go cold, and then she turns and walks down the hallway toward the stairwell, and I close the door because it's 3am and I'm an idiot who values eight more hours of sleep over basic human decency, and that's the TIFU. Thought that was the whole story. It was not the whole story.

Okay so here's where it stops being funny.

I couldn't get back to sleep, kept thinking about the "he doesn't answer anymore" thing, so around 4am I get up for water and I hear the knocking again. Same soft, polite knock. I look through the peephole this time, actually use it like a functioning adult, and there's nobody there. Hallway's empty. I can still hear the knocking.

It's coming from inside my apartment.

I want to be clear I am not a person who believes in this stuff. I've made fun of every one of my roommates for their ghost stories. But I traced the sound and it's coming from the wall behind my bookshelf, this slow, even knock-knock-knock, exactly the rhythm the woman used on my front door, and when I put my hand flat on the drywall it was ice cold, colder than anything else in the room, cold like touching a window in January.

I did the reasonable thing, which was leave the apartment immediately and sit in my car in the parking garage typing this on my phone at 4:40 in the morning like a lunatic.

Here's the part I can't explain and genuinely need help with. I looked up my building's history because obviously I did, and apartment 4C — not mine, the one two doors down that's been "under renovation" the entire eight months I've lived here, never once seen a contractor go in or out — belonged to an elderly man who died alone, and according to one dusty local news archive, wasn't found for close to three weeks. Neighbors said they never once heard a peep out of him in the month before. Article doesn't say his wife's name. Doesn't say if he had one.

The knocking from my wall stopped about ten minutes ago. I know because I've had my phone timer running since I got in the car, which I realize sounds insane, but I needed something to look at that wasn't the dark stairwell of my own building.

It just started again. Three knocks. Then nothing. Then three more, slower, like whoever's doing it is listening for something in between.

I don't want to go back up there. I also don't want to be the guy who calls 911 to report a ghost knocking on his wall, because I can already hear how that call goes. But my wallet, my keys, my whole life is in that apartment, and it's almost 5am, and the garage is starting to feel exactly as unsafe as the hallway did.

Anyway. If anyone's got a number for whoever handles this kind of thing that isn't a priest or a Ouija board — genuinely asking, not being cute — I would take it right about now. My phone's at 9%, the knocking just picked up again, closer this time, and I really don't think it's coming from the wall anymore.

I think it's coming from my front door.
