- built custom landing page for my eBook "Design Faster, Design Better": https://designfaster.netlify.app/
- purpose: drive sales + explain what the book covers; creators increasingly ditching Gumroad default pages
- 7-step process; free; mine needed HTML/CSS/Tailwind, but no-code routes exist
