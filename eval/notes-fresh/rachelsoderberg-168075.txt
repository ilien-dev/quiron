- no kids, split costs with partner; ~$1,800/month spare; 110,000/1,800 = 61.11 months = 5.1 yr goal (ignoring interest)
- tip: pay above the minimum, or high-interest balances keep growing
- expanded a budget spreadsheet I'd barely used
- snowball (Dave Ramsey https://www.daveramsey.com/blog/how-the-debt-snowball-method-works) vs avalanche (Nerd Wallet https://www.nerdwallet.com/blog/finance/what-is-a-debt-avalanche/); chose avalanche, fine with slow wins
- credit cards first, student loan minimums only; credit score rose; moved balances to 0% APR (12-18 mo) offers; no interest-bearing card debt by early 2019
- private loan rate 11.13%, grew $1-2k on minimums; paid off June 2019 with more 0% transfers
- living costs lower than expected; new goal 3 years incl. interest; ~$35k paid by year end; forecast 2.9 years
- caveats: don't let 0% promos lapse; consolidation didn't suit me but valid option
