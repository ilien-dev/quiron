- video: comic, sarcastic walkthrough of WWI's opening, from 1914 through the onset of trench warfare
- opening irony: 1914 pitched as peak of civilization, followed by a quip that a war would be nice
- background causes: France wants territory back from Germany; Germany covets more and builds a showy fleet that unsettles Britain
- peoples inside both empires resent rule; some pursue independence, Russia assisting
- military technology had leaped forward since the previous big war, so all sides itchy to fight
- Sarajevo: Archduke Franz Ferdinand tours by convertible-style car, route announced beforehand; local Serbs and Bosnians despise Austro-Hungarian rule
- first attempt: bombs thrown at his car miss, injure officers in the vehicle behind; he refuses to flee, goes to visit them in hospital
- Austria-Hungary blames Serbia's government (creator: possibly true), secures German support, sends impossible demands, declares war when refused
- alliance chain: Russia protects Serbia and is friends with France; Montenegro joins; Britain answers France's request with a hesitant "Maybe...?" and sits out initially
- German plan: Russia too big and clumsy to mobilize fast, so crush France quickly, then shift east; joke about everyone speaking German and eating Pfefferpotthast
- snag: French border heavily fortified, so Germany detours via neutral Belgium with 750 thousand troops, hoping for meek compliance
- Belgium fights back, and well, slowing the Germans; Britain outraged at the invasion of a neutral country, declares war on Germany
- German atrocities during advance; jokes on spiked helmets and skulls on uniforms, sarcastic praise for not seeming like villains
- Allied propaganda sways world opinion, especially in the US; Woodrow Wilson, fancying himself Jesus-like, mostly urges everyone to reconcile
- German-Americans cheered at the outset, enthusiasm dropped after Belgium
- "Spot the French soldier" gag: bright blue coat, red trousers; Germans easily shredded slow marching columns with big guns; every army had to rework uniforms and tactics
- Russia mobilizes much earlier than expected; Austria-Hungary's promise to deal with it flops; Germany sends troops back east
- Austro-Hungarian chief of staff: creator calls him handsome but weak strategist; ignores German advice, then runs back for help; little Serbia even beats off its early invasions
- in the north, Germany almost wipes out Russia's second army
- west: Germans within sight of Paris, quick win seems likely; French commander-in-chief halts the retreat, a gap opens in German lines, Germans pull back, Allies counter-attack
- both sides entrench, then extend lines toward the sea in repeated attempts to flank one another; trenches run from coast to Switzerland
- trench warfare: days of shelling, shell shock, infantry dashing across muddy no-man's land of craters and barbed wire, machine guns, successive waves, months of fighting and thousands dead for tiny gains
- trench life: corpses, mud that engulfs, poisonous water, rats, disease, stench; creator finds it insane that millions endured it for years
