- topic: Oecophylla weaver ants, pitched as the fantasy-elf aristocrats of the ant realm; kingdoms suspended in tropical jungle foliage
- looks: leggy, slender, large-eyed, kind of cute; powerful jaws and acid-shooting make them effective killers (promised for later)
- worker classes: typically two or three very different sizes (majors, minors, occasionally minute "minim" workers); body color ranges from deep brown through vivid green depending on species and region
- building heights: low shrubs just above ground level up to roughly 10 m high in the treetops
- one plant is never enough; twigs and lianas act as bridges to other trees, so the colony sprawls up and sideways through the canopy
- largest known realm: 1,600 m², roughly four basketball courts; too big to control easily, hence dozens of scattered nests: border outposts, leaf tubes or balls, ghostly silk sheets
- majors handle risky tasks: combat, foraging, nest building
- construction steps: major folds leaves into a tube; helpers form chains, tugging edges together or bridging gaps to catch distant leaves; others bring larvae from the closest existing nest
- larvae donate every bit of silk rather than making their own cocoon; workers tap larval heads on the leaf to release sticky thread; creator's joke: tiny cute glue guns
- nest roles: barracks along the frontier, or supply depots for young and food; keeps soldiers near possible conflict rather than far from headquarters
- one lone nest at the network's center holds the queen and her bodyguards; she lays hundreds of eggs per day, moved on to nests with brood rooms
- colony imagery: castles, moats, suspension bridges of leaves, lianas and twigs; established colony easily numbers around 500,000 mouths
- main rivals are other weaver kingdoms; creator compares queens to medieval humans seeking conquest; controlling good territory decides survival, and losing too much leads to shrinking, takeover or starvation
- attack: a force of several thousand majors heads to the neighboring colony to seize a slice of land; defending patrols detect them, emit alarm pheromone, mark trails to outposts, and shake their bodies to call sisters to the front
- fight: majors from both sides rise up, circle with jaws open, grab enemies, pin them in groups, then dismember (antennae, legs, abdomens slit); both sides spray formic acid; reinforcements from outposts arrive within minutes and the defenders drive the attackers off
- result: heaps of dead, many badly hurt, defenders' nests and young unharmed; attackers will return soon; war is routine; ends with line that empires never get enough, then *quack*
