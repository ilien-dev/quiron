# Where the candidates came from: the brief's starting list (hook, promise, question answered at
# once, recap, stock transitions, the close), the judges' grouped reasons in
# eval/results/script-reasons.txt, and reading ten human train captions and ten assistant train
# scripts in full. The rates that decide are those of held-out humans (49) and of the five test
# folders (45 scripts); train rates are shown beside them.
#
# Verdict rule (the one the script prints): FAIL if at most 5% of held-out humans and at least
# 30% (3 of 9) of some test model; TELL if humans use it and the pooled AI test rate is at least
# twice the held-out human rate and twice the train human rate; out otherwise.
# A FAIL that would fail a held-out human script that is clean today is reported as a choice and
# shipped as a TELL. A rule that needs the video's title cannot run in audit.py, which never sees it.
