# references/script.md as join tested it (deleted after this run):
#   # Video scripts: keep the narrator's sentences
#
#   When a rewrite goes wrong on a script, it is usually because the rewriter cut long sentences into short ones and made them all the same length. People who write narration do the opposite. Ten human captions rewritten with this skill went from 18.9 to 15.3 words a sentence in English and from 19.9 to 15.7 in Spanish (`eval/results/script-e2e.txt`, `script-es-e2e.txt`).
#
#   ## English
#
#   The band is `scripts/bands-script.json`. Human narration runs 13.1 to 25.4 words a sentence, median 17.8, and assistant scripts sit at 12.3.
#
#   - Keep the draft's sentences whole unless the meter flags them. Never split a sentence to fix something else.
#   - Short sentences in a row that make one point get joined with "and", "but" or "so" until the mean sentence length is inside the band. Stop at 17.8.
#   - Keep the narrator's "I" and "we".
#   - Do not add fillers, typos or ellipses.
#
#   ## Spanish
#
#   The band is `scripts/bands-script-es.json`. Human narration runs 14.6 to 29.1 words a sentence, median 21.1, and assistant scripts sit at 14.4.
#
#   - Keep the draft's sentences whole unless the meter flags them. Never split a sentence to fix something else.
#   - Short sentences in a row that make one point get joined with "y", "pero" or "así que" until the mean sentence length is inside the band. Stop at 21.1.
#   - Keep the narrator's "yo" and "nosotros".
