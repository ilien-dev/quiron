## Verdict (written for the documentation; every number is in a section below, found by its command)
# What the end-to-end runs showed for Spanish video scripts, in four findings. P(AI) is the Claude judge's
# mean (column "Opus", Sonnet 5.5 here), 20 titles per set, two rewrites per snapshot unless a row says one.
# 1. The register does not move the judges beyond what the skill on main already did.
#    scr-es: main 69.5, the shipped tree (ship) 69.4 (-0.1, worse on 9 of 20, p=0.778; section "Ship against main").
#    The band files alone against main: +1.1 with the rebuilt snapshot bands2 (p=0.920, section "Ship against main"), -0.5 with the earlier bands snapshot (p=0.295). The shipped checks (ship) against the same tree with them off (bands2): -1.1 (p=0.408,
#    noise 2.8); on scr2-es 69.9 against 70.2 (-0.3, p=0.795). Any rewrite does cut an untouched draft, 79.4 to 69.4 on scr-es
#    and 81.5 to 69.9 on scr2-es, and 88% and 98% of the shipped rewrites are still judged AI (over 50).
# 2. No rule for the shape of a script held. Two sets were tried against the band files alone, both on whole narrations.
#    The pattern rules (one rule per measured pattern) against bands, the earlier snapshot (not bands2): 69.0 to 74.2, +5.2 (worse on 17 of 20, p=0.018; noise 2.1).
#    The same rules without the sentence-length bullets: +2.0 (1 rep). The short rule "keep sentences whole, join short ones
#    up to the band median" (join): +1.8 against the shipped tree (worse on 14 of 20, p=0.167). All three scored higher
#    (the join difference is inside the noise of 2.8) while they brought overshot down (0.80 to 0.45 for the rules, 0.70 to 0.45
#    for join) and sentence length into the band (join).
#    So no references/script.md ships.
# 3. Rewriting a script a person wrote costs it. Ten held-out human captions, one rewrite with the shipped tree: 8.1 to 15.2
#    (+7.1, worse on 8 of 10, p=0.012), GPT judge 11.6 to 29.5; mean sentence length 19.9 to 15.2, CV 0.634 to 0.521. None over 50.
#    The rewriter shortens and evens sentences, against what the bands ask for narration.
# 4. The rewrites that ship overshoot more than the untouched drafts: features past the human band per text, 0.40 to 0.70 on
#    scr-es and 0.30 to 0.82 on scr2-es.

# references/script.md as join tested it (deleted after this run):
#   # Video scripts: keep the narrator's sentences
#
#   When a rewrite goes wrong on a script, it is usually because the rewriter cut long sentences into short ones and made them all the same length. People who write narration do the opposite. Ten human captions rewritten with this skill went from 18.9 to 15.3 words a sentence in English and from 19.9 to 15.7 in Spanish (`eval/results/script-e2e.txt`, `script-es-e2e.txt`).
#
#   ## English
